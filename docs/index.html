How it works

The training pictures were taken by the game itself: a World Editor map places random units, takes a screenshot and writes down their names. A convnext_tiny model was fine-tuned on them with fastai, exported to ONNX and runs here with onnxruntime-web. It looks at the battlefield only, without the game's top bar and bottom panel. Small units like Peons and Ghouls are the hardest for it.
